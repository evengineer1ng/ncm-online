# Getting Started

This page takes you from seeing **NCM Online — Motorsports in Night City** in the OPEN//77 launcher to finishing
your first race. It takes about ten minutes plus download time.

## 1. What you need

- **Cyberpunk 2077 on PC**, at the version OPEN//77 currently supports. OPEN//77 tells you if yours does not match.
- **The OPEN//77 launcher.** It installs and starts multiplayer Cyberpunk. See [open2077.net](https://open2077.net).
- **A keyboard and mouse, a controller, or a wheel.** All three work. A wheel is optional; see
  [Wheel & Rig Support](systems/rig.md).
- **No manual mod installs.** NCM's mods are delivered by the launcher (step 3).

> **Back up your saves first.** OPEN//77 is a multiplayer layer over a single-player game. Copy your Cyberpunk save
> folder somewhere safe before your first session; it is good practice with any game mod.

## 2. Find NCM Online

Open the OPEN//77 launcher and look for **NCM Online** in the server list. It appears with the description
*Motorsports in Night City*. Select it and choose to join.

## 3. Accept NCM's required mods

NCM needs some client-side mods: its own native plugins (physics and parts, rig input, broadcast presentation, the
radio station and others) and well-known community loaders such as ArchiveXL, TweakXL and Codeware. **The launcher
downloads and installs them for you** when you join.

Before anything installs, the launcher lists every package and says where it comes from. NCM's packages are supplied
by the NCM Online server and marked as not reviewed by OPEN//77. Read the list and accept it to continue. The list
changes over time, so the launcher may download updates on a later join.

The first launch can take a minute or two before the game window appears. If the game shows "press any key", press
it.

## 4. What happens when you connect

You arrive in Night City. The first time, NCM grants you a **starting balance** of eddies (€$). On later joins
you return to where you left.

Each session opens the **HOW TO PLAY NCM** panel. It shows the four steps below, the controls, tips and the latest
patch notes. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>Continue</kbd> to close it.

> **Alpha progression is temporary.** Everything you earn, money, machines, parts and records, resets for NCM 1.0,
> and further resets can happen before then. Race Week also has its own reset rules; see
> [Race Week](systems/race-week.md).

## 5. Your first race in four steps

1. **Open NCM.** Press <kbd>F6</kbd> (or <kbd>Start</kbd> on a controller) for the NCM Menu, go to **MARKET** and
   buy a base car. Every listing is one specific machine; check the price against your balance.
2. **Get your car.** In **MACHINE INDEX** (also <kbd>F10</kbd>), select a car you own and choose **CALL VEHICLE**.
   It appears in a clear space near you. Hold <kbd>E</kbd> near it to get in.
3. **Find a race.** Open **RACE GUIDE** in the F6 menu. Each race shows its route, start time, weather, entry fee,
   payout and whether your car is eligible. Mark one **Interested** to track it and set your route there. That does
   not reserve a place.
4. **Go racing.** Drive to the race's venue, **enter** at the venue, then join the grid. At the grid, <kbd>F7</kbd>
   snaps you to your exact starting spot. When the lights go green, race.

**Racing alone is fine.** NPC racers fill empty grid places around start time. Some events are time trials against
the clock.

## 6. Before your first race

- **Entering costs the listed entry fee**, charged when you enter. Practice and qualifying pay nothing; the race does.
- **Arrive early.** Qualifying or other sessions can start before the scheduled race start. Qualifying helps set grid
  order.
- **Changed your mind?** Use **Withdraw** at the venue before the race starts. Once it has started, **Forfeit**
  (`/ncm.forfeit`, asked twice) retires you and records a DNF.
- **Damage persists.** Crashes cost condition, which costs money or work to put right. See
  [Engineering](systems/engineering.md).
- **Watch your fuel.** The level is under the odometer. See [Fuel](systems/fuel.md).
- **Rain matters.** Wet roads mean less grip and longer braking until they dry.

## 7. Controls that matter

| Control | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| NCM Menu (Race Guide, Market, Machine Index, Messages, Help...) | <kbd>F6</kbd> | <kbd>Start</kbd> |
| Quick menu (Hub, Race graph, Call a race, Map, GPS) | hold <kbd>B</kbd>, aim, release | hold <kbd>Start</kbd>, aim, release |
| Engineering (service, parts, Workshop) | <kbd>F9</kbd> | via the NCM Menu |
| Machine Index | <kbd>F10</kbd> | via the NCM Menu |
| Live race graph while driving | <kbd>F8</kbd> | — |
| Snap to your grid spot | <kbd>F7</kbd> at the grid | — |
| Call a custom street race | <kbd>F5</kbd> | via the quick menu |
| Scanner (discover machines) | <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>Caps Lock</kbd> | — |
| Close any NCM panel | <kbd>Esc</kbd> | <kbd>Back</kbd> |

Menus work without a mouse: arrow keys move focus, <kbd>Enter</kbd> selects. On a controller the D-pad navigates and
Confirm selects. The full list is on [Controls & Commands](commands.md).

## 8. If something doesn't work

1. Check [Known Issues](known-issues.md). It may be known, with a workaround.
2. **Report it from inside the game:** F6 → **HELP** → report a problem. The report goes to the server with the
   context the page lists (for example which screen was open and the server build), so you need no other account.
3. Or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/evengineer1ng/ncm-online/issues/new/choose). Never paste
   passwords, tokens or other private information into an issue.
