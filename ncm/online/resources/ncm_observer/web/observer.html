Watching the races

NCM OBSERVER · HOW IT WORKS
  1. F3 opens the Observer panel. ENTER OBSERVER turns you into an invisible flying camera. Step out of your car first. Nothing you do while observing counts: no racing, no money.
  2. Your team's drivers are marked in gold: in YOUR TEAM and RACES NOW, in the running order, and down here on the screen.
  3. Press Y to jump to your drivers, one after another. If none is racing, it tells you, and the gold appears as soon as one starts.
  4. Click a race, then a driver to watch. Or press N and the director picks the story: it holds a shot and cuts to battles.
  5. 1-8 camera · PgUp/PgDn the car ahead or behind · Home the leader · B the closest battle · H a clean screen.
  6. Green dot: the car is really on the road. Grey: it is still in the simulation and arrives as the camera gets close.
  7. EXIT OBSERVER (F3) puts you back where you started.
