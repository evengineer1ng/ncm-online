NIGHT CITY MOTORSPORTS // ONLINE RACING

HOW TO PLAY NCM

Your first race is four steps away.
01

OPEN NCM

Open the NCM Menu with F6 and buy a base car from the Market.

You can upgrade, trade and build from there.
02

GET YOUR CAR

Go to Machine Index and call a car you own.

It tracks your machines, ownership and what you have learned about them.
03

FIND A RACE

Use Race Guide to find an event that interests you.

Races cross Night City, with different routes, fields, entry fees and payouts.
04

GO RACING

Travel to the race node, enter, grid up and race.

Racing alone? NPC racers fill the grid around event start time.
