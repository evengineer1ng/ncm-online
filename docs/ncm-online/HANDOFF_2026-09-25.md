### The governing principle from L123, which resolves most ambiguity

A car is a machine asset. A component is a component asset. "Team car", "grid car", "street car" describe use,

### The A-series rulings (L124)

- **A1 — `origin` is retired conceptually.** It does not earn its place as an independent machine concept, and
  **you must not invent `entry_route` to replace it.** The meaningful facts already exist elsewhere: production
  identity/ordinal describes legitimate production and serialisation; acquisition/provenance
  (`acquired_via`) describes how ownership was acquired; `grant` as a *species* of machine is obsolete.
  **This is a semantic ruling, not a migration instruction.** `origin` is `NOT NULL DEFAULT 'production'` on a
  live table. Determine the safest retirement mechanics — and whether anything must replace the column at all —
  **before** any production change.
- **A2 — LOCKED. Team machines use the same serial sequences.** An NCX machine consumes the same
  manufacturer/model ordinals as any other legitimate machine. **No NCX serialisation universe, no asset
  exception.** Exclusivity belongs in **access and distribution**, not ontology. The owner path is:
  qualify/control organization → restricted market/distribution access → acquire → manufacturer supply consumed
  → ordinary machine asset → owned by the organization principal. This **promotes qualified/restricted market
  access to load-bearing architecture** (`IDEA-298`). `player_market_knowledge.access_state` exists and is
  unused — evaluate it, do not ignore it.
- **A4 — historical attribution must preserve epistemic basis.** Do **not** rewrite the eight post-purchase
  results as though MaiMai attribution had been recorded contemporaneously. Accepted vocabulary:
  **`recorded`** (captured contemporaneously) · **`derived`** (established afterward from preserved evidence
  under an explicit derivation rule) · **`unknown`**. A derived attribution must stay **permanently
  distinguishable** from recorded fact, and should carry enough derivation provenance to explain *why* it was
  derived — not a naked flag. Driver A's two results and all other unsupported history remain **unknown**.
- **A5 — LOCKED. Consequence-pending conditions are acceptable.** A condition may exist authoritatively and be
  surfaced to the player before its validated mechanical consequence is enabled. **This does not make wear
  cosmetic.** The model remains *authoritative condition → eventual validated consequence*. Do not invent fake
  effects to make an interim implementation look finished, and do not let information-only become the permanent
  product model.

## 7. The architectural compression — the most important finding

**Much of the desired NCM world is already designed.** A large part of the apparent feature surface compresses
into a few missing shared primitives:

```
owner principal  →  physical asset  →  custody  →  transfer envelope
```

These underpin team ownership, team treasuries, machine and component ownership, storage, installation/removal,
market custody, mixed transactions, classifieds and future service relationships.

The root blocker: **NCM has no principal that can hold title, money or custody — everything is keyed to a
driver.** `vehicle_asset.ncm_driver_id` and `ledger.ncm_driver_id` are both `NOT NULL` with FKs to `ncm_driver`.
Migration 015 already named `owner_principal_kind/id` in a comment as "a cheap later ALTER precisely because the
primary key is the asset rather than the owner" — the foresight exists and was never spent. Filed as
**`IDEA-297`**.

**Do not implement these merely because this prompt names them.** Re-read `24_FOUNDATION_RECONCILIATION.md` §3
(primitives), §4 (contradictions) and §9 (sequence) first.

**Also preserve this constraint:** `setPerformance` is **one profile per vehicle and does not stack**, and class
homologation ceilings already consume it (`IDEA-079`/`IDEA-102`). Component wear and upgrades therefore **cannot
assume an independently stackable performance layer**. The engine-capability/effect audit (`IDEA-249`) must
resolve this *before* mechanical component effects are designed — it is an input to that audit, not a discovery
to be made during it.
