# WP M3.1 — The schedule exists (posted races, pings, venue entry)

Milestone: M3. Status: **complete 2026-09-19** (live at 20:38; polish from tester feedback awaiting one restart). Depends on M2.1/M2.2. Decisions: L34 (scheduled start times are the grammar), L35 (entry is a prompted act at the venue), L37 (frequent small races), P17–P21.

## What it does
- **Core:** SP nodes and roots become core data (`data.nodes`, `data.roots`); `course.routes` resolves a root to start/finish nodes; `competition.calendar.materialize` turns a series (cadence, routes, entry window) into posted slots for a horizon with deterministic event ids; `competition.entry` decides check-in (window open, at venue, not already entered) and which pings are due.
- **ncm_league (server):** content pack #1 v0 (`street_sprint`: every 20 min, rotating Tip-to-Trench / Snake in the Grass / B-line), scheduler tick materialises the next 2 h, persists `event`/`entry` (migration 003), publishes the next events on `GlobalState.ncmCalendar`, sends T−15/−5/−1 pings to connected drivers, and handles `ncm:enter` (re-validates the venue distance server-side, writes the `checked_in` entry).
- **ncm_world (client):** heartbeat hint ("NEXT RACE · Tip-to-Trench sprint · 12:34 · 1.8 km") as a persistent `open77_notifications` card, a world marker at the venue with the prompt **"Pay entry fee and enter?"** (hold E, or hold X on a pad) → server; `/ncm.gps` tracks a routable blip so the minimap draws the road route.

## Completion criteria
1. Server log shows the street series materialised (≥3 slots ahead) and `GlobalState.ncmCalendar` published.
2. In-game: heartbeat hint counts down to the next slot and shows distance; T−15/−5/−1 toasts arrive.
3. Driving to the venue shows the marker + prompt; confirming yields "You're in" and an `entry` row with `state=checked_in`; a second confirm says already entered; confirming from >30 m away or outside the window is refused server-side.
4. Slot passes → event closes (`state=closed`, entries frozen) and the hint moves to the next slot.
5. Tests green under both interpreters; vendoring clean; SP untouched.

## Out of scope
Grid/start/gates/timing (M3.2), settlement (M3.3), AI backfill (M5), short-notice player events (P17; after M3.2).
