# OPEN//77 Week vehicle custody and damage check

> **STATUS: HISTORICAL** - read-only API research for L349; no platform experiment passed.

On 2026-10-08 the current official [server vehicle API](https://open2077.net/docs/api/server/open77-vehicles) and [vehicle guide](https://open2077.net/docs/vehicles) document `getDamage`/`setDamage`, `getHealth`, `repair`, `isDoorOpen`/`openDoor` and `Open77.vehicles.doors.trunk`. The canonical damage read includes the 30-cell body grid and glass, light, tyre and detached-part masks. `setDamage` validates its input. The guide says removing an already detached part bit is refused because no safe reattachment primitive is validated; a fresh Week vehicle instance avoids claiming that shop work reattached it.

The official [loot guide](https://open2077.net/docs/loot) describes server-owned ground drops and pickup authorization, and [player-interaction guide](https://open2077.net/docs/player-interactions) tells separate inventory resources to use their own server exports. Neither gives NCM a generic authoritative vehicle inventory or a way to transfer every vanilla backpack item into a car. Thus L349's first pass moves only NCM serialized components into custody of an open trunk; ordinary items need IDEA-1252's bridge. A client inventory claim cannot establish deposit.
