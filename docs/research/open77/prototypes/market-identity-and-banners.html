Market identity and player banners

Select a market on the left. The markup and the components never change — only a data-market-theme attribute does. That is the whole mechanism: an identity is a scoped token override block, so a themed market costs one CSS block and zero WebUI surfaces, which matters because NCM has one spare surface in its budget of eight. Market names are placeholders.

1 · One shell, many identities

Your access
// NCM COMMERCIAL BASELINE

Base Market

Open to every driver. Serialized machines and components, priced plainly, no relationship required.

Note what did not move: the six-tab nav, the panel frame, the price column and the purchase affordance. A theme may set an accent, a plate fill, a frame motif, a texture, a type role, a motion duration and a copy register. It may not touch the shell, the money figure, or the legibility floors. An unknown theme inherits every base token, so a market with missing art still opens.

2 · The boundary, stated

LayerThemeable
Shell nav, frame, back, focus ringsNever — the player must always know whose software they are in

Price and commit affordanceNever — money does not take on a local dialect
9px floor, 0.94 opacity, contrast, tabular numeralsNever — a theme may not opt out of being readable

Resolution order

Explicit themeId → operator/organisation → specialisation → relationship → supply tier → clientele → district as a flavour modifier only → base.

Geography is never the primary key, so two markets in one district can differ and one operator can carry its identity across districts. This matches 17_MARKET_NETWORK.md, which already rejects name-as-identity and calls display_name "presentation, independently mutable" against an opaque market_id.

288×84, grid minmax(0,1fr) 82px, art composited at opacity .72 under the name, bottom-left 8px clipped — all measured from the shipped nameplates.css, not invented. The consequence is the format rule: a banner is a background wash, not a picture. Author at ~2.45:1, let crop frame it, and expect text on top. The banner is the ground; the badges are the marks on it. Worn banners stay static — this page runs one card per streamed player at 60fps, so animation belongs to the unlock, which happens once.

5 · What the code delta actually is

Already built in driver.presentationMissing
Banner registry with stable ids, a theme field, an entitlement reason, image + crop, entitlement computation, selection validated with typed issues (unknown_banner, banner_not_entitled), persistence in driver_presentation.banner_id, a safe public projection, a client asset catalogue, and a working renderer. One write-once provenance table (banner_grant: banner id, source_kind, source_ref, first_granted_at) and a second input to M.entitlements(). The selection, validation and projection path needs no change at all.

Which is why this is a small piece of work rather than a new system: banners already exist in embryo and have no earning path. Implement source_kind = "market" only; the other sources (venue, district, championship, manufacturer, team, organisation, event, milestone) are proven possible by the column existing. No banner ever grants money, grip, access or standing — the moment one does, players optimise collection instead of enjoying it, and every future banner becomes a balance argument.
